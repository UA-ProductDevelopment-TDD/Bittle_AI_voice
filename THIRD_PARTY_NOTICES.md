## `dist/sounds/growl.ogg`

- Titel: “Dogs' Expectation about Signalers' Body Size by Virtue of Their Growls” — geluidsfragment van een grote hond
- Auteurs: Faragó T, Pongrácz P, Miklósi Á, Huber L, Virányi Z en Range F
- Bronbestand: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dogs%27-Expectation-about-Signalers%27-Body-Size-by-Virtue-of-Their-Growls-pone.0015175.s007.ogg
- Onderzoek: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0015175
- Licentie: Creative Commons Attribution 2.5 Generic — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/

## `dist/sounds/sniff.ogg`

- Titel: “004 - Dog Sniffing.wav”
- Auteur: Trashcan_Studios
- Bron: https://freesound.org/people/Trashcan_Studios/sounds/580218/
- Licentie: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
